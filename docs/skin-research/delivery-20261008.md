## Surface shine method and failure evidence

`surface-specular-candidate-v1` uses the actual anatomical mesh/exclusions, conservative
appearance sampling, fixed brightness/saturation and positive contrast against a local
11x11 valid-neighbor median. Candidate >=0.15 is a DEVELOPMENT parameter, not a universal
clinical threshold. There is no per-image min/max or invented healthy/unhealthy range.
Numerator: candidate valid grid samples. Denominator: all valid grid samples. Reported
proportion is an equal-area grid approximation of visible candidate area, not sebum
amount, moisture, secretion or oil percentage. Grid longest dimension <=128; no lesion
localization claim is supported by this resolution.

Appearance mask excludes dark/saturated/non-skin-color samples conservatively and
uses eye/lip geometry. It is not a validated tissue segmentation model: beard, makeup,
deeply shadowed/dark skin and uncommon illumination remain confounds. Overexposure
uses pre-exclusion source clipping fraction >0.03 to return unavailable rather than
calling glare oil. Eye surroundings are outside oil scope.

Real licensed three-pose video frames: all five region proportions were zero for both
baseline and current method. Repeated captures had identical source hashes because
this is a repeating fixture; they are NOT physical repeatability evidence.

model landmarks were also evaluated. They are only front-source engineering evidence.

## Dryness and sagging preparation

Zero real expert annotation rows were supplied; preparation reports zero and training
refuses. `annotation-protocol.json` defines visible flaking vs physiological moisture,
and neutral-pose contour laxity vs mechanical elasticity. No arbitrary landmark,
brightness, texture or skin-scan hydration score is produced. Capture specifies actual
native unmirrored RGB, resolution, fixed/recorded lighting/exposure/white balance,
neutral expression, pose, repeat/lighting challenge and camera-domain metadata.
At least two independent expert grades, third-expert adjudication when needed, valid
regional boxes and source-coordinate invalid/target polygons are required. Rights
include actual license-text path/hash, commercial training and weight distribution
permissions, consent scope/withdrawal and source photo hash. Boolean pseudo-grades,
AI/synthetic targets, duplicate reviewer IDs, mismatching consent/hash/dimensions and
out-of-frame polygons are rejected. Case partitions and exact/near duplicate leakage
are checked; repeat photos cannot stand in for independent cases.
Future ordinal training from scratch produces regional expert grade probabilities;
evaluation reports confusion, MAE and weighted kappa. Regional grade alone never
creates a localization map. Expert agreement and camera-domain acceptance are still
required. Prototype grade anchors require expert confirmation; they are not a validated
moisture or elasticity instrument. Existing Info/reasons remain the only user explanation.
